# Claim-level annotation guidelines

Annotate one atomic, independently checkable factual claim at a time.

## Labels
- **SUPPORTED**: the inspected cited source directly supports the claim's material meaning.
- **PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED**: the source supports only a subset, weaker version, narrower scope, or qualified form.
- **NOT_SUPPORTED_IN_INSPECTED_SOURCE**: the relevant source was inspected and does not provide support. This does not prove the claim false.
- **CONTRADICTED**: the inspected source provides evidence inconsistent with the claim.
- **UNANNOTATED**: no defensible label has been assigned.

## Evidence availability (separate field)
Use `available`, `not_retrieved`, `inaccessible`, or `not_provided`. If a source cannot be inspected, do not label the claim unsupported or contradicted merely because evidence is missing.

Record exact evidence passages and source locations. Use at least two independent annotators on a pilot subset, measure agreement, discuss disagreements, revise the codebook and adjudicate.
